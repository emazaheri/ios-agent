# Cards (com.example.cards)

Written against the scripted build in `tests/evals/agent/screens.py`.

The bar along the bottom is how this app navigates, and its three glyphs carry
no labels. Left to right they are `tabbar_item_1` (Discover, the cards),
`tabbar_item_2` (Likes) and `tabbar_item_3` (Me, the profile).

Everything about the account sits under Me. The app calls its notification
settings **Nudges**, not Notifications, and Quiet Hours is a switch on that
screen.
